# Life game modules
- Lawgivers legislature: cycle-free legislature-schema.ts (zod-only, embedded as optional career.legislature) holds chamber seating, 14 named key lawmakers, aggregate backbench blocs, bills and the 3-slot legislative docket; legislation.ts ensureLegislature/ensureLegislativeDocket generate them deterministically once per tenure/quarter without touching money, approval or actions. It does not yet drive evaluateSupport vote counts.
- Executive slot 1 draws from legislativePool (legislation.ts) by a tenure-seeded quarterly rotation with Sign/Veto/Return only; pool bills create no Bill records and change approval groups only, and returned bills persist in executiveDocket.returned (carried by initializeDocket) to reappear revised two quarters later. Slot 2 projects use contractProject: clean, steer (kickback + corruption exposure + seeded scandal) or siphon (treasury→personal, Severe scandal, formal legal case), reusing corruption/scandal/legal records. approvalDrift applies a seeded 2–4 point incumbency fatigue each quarter. Slot 2 shows the annual budget every Q4 (chamber vote via legislativeBudgetVote; failure imposes balanced) and cabinet nominations in Q2 or after a rejection (executiveDocket.renominate rotates to a different unfilled post). court.ts grandCorruption (siphon|/steer| txn ids) forces conviction, removal, full forfeiture and life (siphon; death for a president) or the maximum statutory term (steer); legal.ts blocks counsel delays/defense, evidence motions and political dismissal for it.
- Executive docket uses cycle-free docket-schema.ts for legacy initialization and executive-docket.ts for frozen quarterly references and revision-guarded Results; Game dispatches against the latest career, turn gating applies only to active non-detained executives, and decisions delegate to existing chamber/appointment/project accounting so no parallel enactment or treasury path exists.
- Civilian job catalog and credential/performance checks live in browser-safe job-market.ts; gameplay re-exports the catalog for compatibility and uses existing application records for immediate offers/rejections, preserving pending legacy decisions. Monthly skill gains and full-quarter reputation bonuses share the salary watermark; workMonths remains lifetime experience and employment.ts uses each role's primary skill for seniority pay.
- Employment pay lives in employment.ts: existing lifetime work thresholds drive persisted pay levels and performance-scaled raises once at quarter end; shared pay/forecast helpers power displays, bank income and next-quarter itemized personal wages, while optional job fields preserve legacy records without backpay.
- Personal banking uses banking-schema.ts and pure banking.ts; Career.money remains checking, nullable banking preserves legacy wealth, revision guards reject stale requests and an end watermark settles savings/CDs/loans once per quarter before living costs (also in custody); only personal ledgers/accounts change.
- General-election debates use debates.ts and debate-schema.ts; save the invitation before newspaper publication and guard each choice with encounter identity/round so reloads cannot replay support, approval or social effects.
- Housing lives in housing.ts (catalog + startingHousing by background); career.ts stores nullable housing and backfills it in parseCareer, so legacy saves never need migration code elsewhere.
- Housing transactions (rentProperty/buyProperty/collectRent) are pure Result functions that touch only career.money and the personal ledger; collectRent is idempotent via housing.lastRentPeriod (move-in prepays the current quarter) so switching or reloading never double-bills; advanceTurn calls it once with the turn start date after income.
- Health and diet live in life.ts; parseCareer backfills via ensureLife so old saves keep progress, and diet and insurance choices are pure Results gated on personal cash covering one quarter, with quarterlyExpenses as the shared cost preview (billing comes later).
- Vehicles and possessions live in vehicles.ts (catalog, starter transport by background, normalizeVehicleState); ensureLife normalizes saved life.vehicles/activeVehicleId/possessions so legacy saves load with empty state, and no active vehicle means the transit pass.
- Vehicle/possession transactions (buy/sell/service/setActive/buyPossession) are pure Results in vehicles.ts that touch only career.money and the personal ledger; garage-panel.tsx dispatches them via onResult. Perks (campaignTravelRelief in vitalityDrift, wardrobeReputationBonus in early gameplay checks) are small read-only helpers.
- Quarterly transport (collectTransport in vehicles.ts) runs inside collectLiving, keyed by life.lastTransportPeriod; unpaid upkeep sets life.maintenanceOverdue, which suspends vehicle perks.
- Personal social circle lives in relationships.ts (archetype catalog, startingSocial by background, normalizeSocial) under life.social, normalized by ensureLife; it never reads or writes political contacts, relationships or groups.
- Circle & Family display helpers (tiers, standing text, allowances) live in social.ts and are read-only; social-panel.tsx dispatches relationships.ts Results via onResult.
- Social mechanics (interactions, meeting people, dating, proposal, wedding) are pure Results in relationships.ts that touch only career.money, career.actions, life.stress, reputation (wedding) and the personal ledger; each person can be seen once per turn via contact.lastInteraction, and proposal needs partner.datingSince before the current turn.
- Family costs are rule constants per quarter (familyCosts: married household + flat per-child), not the saved partner/dependent cost fields, which stay only for save compatibility; familyStressRelief is a read-only helper for turn processing.
- Quarterly family processing (collectFamily in family.ts) runs in advanceTurn right after collectLiving, keyed by life.social.lastFamilyPeriod; family-image voter/campaign effects fire only on transitions of social.familyImage so they never stack. It is not called from life.ts to avoid an import cycle with campaign.ts.
- Starting background balance lives in BACKGROUND_CONFIG (career.ts) and is read only by createCareer; parseCareer and advanceTurn never consult it, so saves never receive starting bonuses again.
- Dynasty starting scenarios live in character.ts; createCareer rolls once (injectable rng) and saves career.startingScenario. Scenarios needing player-held appointments resolve to Connected Heir until such offices exist (PLAYER_APPOINTMENTS_SUPPORTED); party roles reuse career.position, never office.
- Companies stay in career.businesses; normalizeBusiness preserves legacy balances and defaults missing catalogs/staff to empty, account aliases operatingCash, and dividends exclude locked capital. business-workforce owns staff and saved GM appointments; business-engine shares deterministic capacity/demand/payroll/tax math with finance projections. Existing normal/prison collectBusinesses hooks settle company-only cash once with independent start/end watermarks, capped performance history and revision invalidation.
- Products use business-products.ts for quality economics, company-only setup costs and price/reputation demand; catalog edits never settle time. GM snapshots use trusted-circle identities or explicitly recruited professional executives; quality changes capacity while active management removes owner-absence penalties.

- Support Groups & Political Loyalty lives in support-groups.ts: six voter blocs with loyalty and party standing kept separate from approval, reputation and campaign endorsement favorability (career.groups); null saved data falls back to a deterministic background-weighted baseline so older saves need no migration. Decision hooks (submitMeasure, approveProject, speech, pledges) apply targeted trade-off tables there; campaign scores/donations read its backing helpers, and advanceTurn drift is guarded by a saved quarter watermark so replays never re-apply.
- Social media lives in social-media.ts (career.socialMedia, nullable for older saves; new fields default via zod): posts spend turn energy (career.actions, POST_COST), with a per-quarter cap and diminishing returns. socialTurn runs once per turn from advanceTurn, guarded by socialMedia.lastTurn, and owns follower growth, mentions, inbox, verification/monetization milestones and creator payouts (personal ledger only, never campaign or treasury); the X-style UI only reads it and marks items read.
- S (the social platform) context DMs live in social-context.ts (type-only imports so career.ts stays light): each election/scandal/bill/project event is keyed once in socialMedia.contextSent within a 3-month window, so replays and older saves never flood the inbox. Spam throttle (batched follows via pendingFollows, one citizen mention per turn) never changes follower growth. Professional Dashboard figures come from read-only social-analytics.ts.
- Feed reactions, broadcast interviews and owned-media coverage live in media.ts (not social-media.ts, which career.ts imports for its schema) so approval/support-group code never enters career.ts; mediaTurn runs after socialTurn guarded by socialMedia.lastMediaTurn, and owned coverage spends only the media business account. A fresh rival attack ad also costs an officeholder 1 approval there.
- Media Hub: media-outlets.ts owns the outlet catalog and mediaStories (recent bills/projects/elections/campaigns/crises/active scandals only); media-hub.ts derives invitations from those stories and persists hubAnswered/hubUsed in socialMedia, so invitations exist only when a real event supports them and answers cannot replay. media.ts may import media-outlets but never media-hub (avoids a cycle).
- Rival candidates as social accounts live in rival-feed.ts: rivalFeedItems and profileFor are fully derived from saved campaign state (polls, endorsements, attack, support/momentum) plus a date hash, so no save fields exist and save/load reproduces the identical feed; the social UI links every post author to these profiles.
- Scandal responses live in scandal-responses.ts (imports scandals/approval/support-groups, never the reverse, to avoid cycles): one saved deterministic response/outcome per scandal through applyActionApproval and shiftLoyalty; quarterly fallout is guarded per scandal by falloutTurn and skips campaign claims already penalized by approvalDrift.
- Corruption lives in corruption.ts/corruption-schema.ts (career.corruption, nullable): bribes credit only the off-books reserve, dark money enters campaign funds only with an 'Undisclosed' line plus an illicit record; offers are once per turn via saved turn markers, and corruptionTurn (lastTurn guard) turns exposure into Phase 4 scandals before scandalsTurn — keeps clean balances isolated and leaks replay-safe.
- Government contracts live in contracts.ts (award flow) with payContractor in public-projects.ts: awards never move money; each paid quarterly milestone credits the contractor business account once via contract.lastPaidEnd. Only direct awards to an owned firm are self-dealing (illicit 'self_dealing' record + exposure); corruptionTurn gives each one whistleblower chance, tracked in corruption.whistled.
- Legal investigations live in legal.ts/legal-schema.ts (career.legal, nullable): cases open only from concrete evidence (corruption exposure, evidenced scandals), advance one stage per quarter in legalTurn (lastTurn guard) after scandal fallout, and never convict; counsel and motions charge only personal cash.
- Laundering, influence peddling and embezzlement live in laundering.ts; laundering is the only reserve→personal path (fee + ledger line + illicit record), embezzlement is executive-only via spendTreasury with a UI confirm, each once per turn — keeps every illicit move traceable.
- Court trials live in court.ts, called only from legalTurn for indicted cases: one saved verdict per case, penalties from personal cash (shortfall = court debt), legal.adjudicated blocks re-trying old evidence, and legal.prison is the flag for Prison Mode — prevents double sentencing and keeps saves valid.
- Court debt (legal.owed) is settled from personal cash inside legalTurn after its lastTurn guard (normal and prison turns), with manual payCourtDebt in Legal and prison panels; no replay on reload.
- Civilian crime remains in crime.ts with permanent crime.records; crimeTurn follows legalTurn before newspaper publication in both ordinary and detained turns to resolve fast trials without serving a newly imposed sentence twice. Remand shares isIncarcerated navigation, police uses isDetained, and bail preserves outside screens; pardons.ts flags canonical records and releases the existing legal.prison without removing history.
- Prison Mode: prison.ts owns isIncarcerated; advanceTurn hands jailed careers to prisonAdvance (family, businesses, legalTurn, paper only; no events), doActivity refuses, and CareerView swaps Politics/Government/Life/Career & Business and Social Media for locked panels — the lock derives from saved legal.prison so it survives reloads.
- Prison activities consume saved career.actions through prison-activities.ts; per-quarter uses and actionRevision reject repeat requests. Prison manager requests delegate to shared GM appointments; completed sentences retain felony/pardon flags independently of capped cases, with conviction.ts enforcing rehabilitation before campaign discovery/launch.
- Scandal consolidation lives in scandals.ts: registerScandal folds same-family allegations (corruption+business together) within CONSOLIDATE_MONTHS into one open case via scandal.related; closeScandals (called by court verdicts) and the quarterly lifecycle set closedAt so resolved cases stop prompts/fallout but stay as history. Only notifications and old newspaper editions are clearable (history.ts); ledgers, elections, terms and legal records never are.
- approval.ts bounds routine shifts ±1 and major shifts ±5; applyActionApproval dampens saved same-turn repeats and never rewards failures.
- Approval drift and office-duty shifts (crises, neglect, legislative results) apply only while an office is held (holdsOffice/dutyApproval in approval.ts); quiet officeholder turns regress overall and party support on separate tracks toward an office baseline — keeps non-officeholders exempt and stats decoupled.
- Debate state and permanent history live in browser-safe debate modules; quarterly scheduling precedes newspaper publication, saved choices resolve after it and social reactions never reroll outcomes.
- Treasury accounting lives in budget.ts with tenure-scoped cash, permanent keyed transactions and a quarterly end watermark; existing authority/allocation checks precede cash debits, inherited reserves net legacy commitments, and turn settlement precedes office expiry so government cash never mixes with personal salary or campaign accounts; treasuryTurn also charges baseline-scaled infrastructure/public-safety upkeep (maintenanceCost, capped at cash) independent of discretionary allocations so reserves cannot pile up, and new public-works/crisis prices come from shared multiplier constants while saved projects keep their recorded cost.
- Town halls live in town-halls.ts with town-hall-schema.ts: the encounter's questions are frozen on the campaign when hosted, a saved quarter list allows one per quarter, and each answer is guarded by encounter id/round; one shared effects helper drives both the answer preview and the applied support-group loyalty/momentum so displayed and actual gains match.
- Strategic advertising lives in campaign.ts; Game dispatches against the latest career with saved ad revision, campaign identity and quarter guards. Ad previews share deterministic campaign-only costs/effects; saved score offsets and bounded Independent penalties feed both polls and elections so attacks are not cosmetic poll overlays. Reputation minus proven-record damage is the integrity proxy because the career has no separate integrity statistic.
- Specialist campaign recruitment/payroll live in campaign.ts; cycle-free campaign-staff-schema.ts owns roster fallback and shared perk helpers for media/debates. Game dispatches recruitment against the latest career with revision/turn/campaign guards. Legacy staff retain original wages and excess aides; director salaries settle before donations with an independent period marker, and unpaid directors leave without debt. Integer polls round researched attack targets only after the exact multiplier preview.
- Annual fiscal cycle lives in fiscal-year.ts: the Q1 docket policy slot adopts a tax policy mapped onto the existing budget tax stance (single revenue source), credits one capped start-of-year replenishment keyed per tenure/year in treasury transactions, and shifts bloc loyalty through support-groups, so reloads cannot replay or overflow the treasury.
- Electoral College: campaign.ts owns swingStates, stateProjections and targetState (optional campaign.stateSwing, capped per state); presidential general settlement saves optional ElectionResult.stateCalls, which electoral-map.tsx animates read-only, so older saves load unchanged.
- Independents use one stateFactor helper in both stateProjections and resolveRace: partisan-lean loyalty penalty plus a player-only breakthrough (campaign raised, this campaign's debate result, battleground stateSwing) damped in partisan states, so the map preview and the real count never diverge.
- Rival pressure: rival-pressure.ts (cycle-free) owns weakSpots/pledgeTargets and damage constants shared by campaign, debates, town halls and rival-feed; opponentsAct attacks frontrunners and leaks each weak spot once via saved campaign.rivalPressure (decaying, capped damage subtracted in scores), so attacks move polls and elections and reloads never replay leaks.
- Post-election transition lives in transition.ts: speech/PAC choices save ElectionResult.transitionChoice on the newest general result so they apply once and survive reloads; the turn-report modal renders choices and CSS balloons with no turn-loop changes.
- Company expansions live in business-expansions.ts (catalog + read-only effects); business.saved expansions (default []) feed quarterlySimulation demand/unit-cost/upkeep, and dividendLimits caps payouts at retained earnings above max(locked capital, next-quarter expenses).
- Essential bills (utilities by home, flat social/healthcare levy, owned-home upkeep) live in obligations.ts; collectLiving calls collectObligations once per life.lastBillsPeriod, shortfall saves life.billArrears (shutoff energy/health hit, credit score penalty in banking.ts) and is repaid with a late surcharge next quarter.
- Personal taxes live in taxes.ts: collectTaxes runs in advanceTurn after collectLiving (kept out of life.ts to avoid a scandals import cycle), taxes positive personal-ledger income for the quarter once via life.lastTaxPeriod, credits career.treasury when present, and records non-honest stances as tax_evasion illicit txns so the existing exposure/scandal/court pipeline handles fallout; pay-focused tests mock collectTaxes.
- Legislature interactions live in pure legislature-actions.ts: directory lobbying (whip/compromise/sponsor) and leadership contests are seeded by tenure+quarter/year and recorded in legislature.interactions/leadershipBids so reloads cannot reroll; won posts persist in legislature.playerRoles. chamber-chart.tsx renders the hemicycle from saved seating only.
- Legislative docket: committees.ts (schema-only) owns the six committees, seat splits and the deterministic rollCall (named lawmakers individually, rank-and-file blocs by party line + regional lean in Congress; projectVote = rollCall with the player's seat voting with the rank-and-file, so a cast vote moves the tally by at most one). legislation.ts ensureLegislativeDocket builds the floor/committee/whip cards; legislative-docket.ts resolves them with docket id+revision guards and an optional player vote. Bills record rollCalls and an origin chamber; bicameral passage goes origin → other chamber next quarter → awaiting_signature. Legislators' desk bills are decided by the seeded npcPresident in advanceTurn (presidentialReview, idempotent by status); presidents get one congressSession per turn (saved watermark) and decide desk bills in executive slot 1 via `leg:` references. Enacted-law bloc fallout and party-rebellion penalties live in approval.ts (applyLegislativeFallout, partyLineFallout). GoverningPanel part="legislative" hosts the docket, floor-vote-modal.tsx and the bill tracker.
- Unrest & impeachment live in impeachment.ts with cycle-free impeachment-schema.ts (optional career.impeachment): impeachmentTurn runs once per quarter after approvalDrift via a saved quarter watermark; unrest (<30%) drains treasury and approval until calm (>=38%) or answered; proceedings (2 quarters <20%, or <25% with a Severe scandal) advance one stage per quarter (mayor: council majority; governor/president: house majority then 2/3 senate), conviction reuses court.removeFromOffice, sets barred (checked in ballotRestriction) and registers a Severe referral scandal.
- Endorsement gating (25% gains without backing), the general-election turnout deficit and independent ballot access live in campaign.ts helpers; the deficit applies only at vote counting so polls/scores stay additive.
- Childbirth, political manager, retirement and heir succession live in pure dynasty.ts with cycle-free dynasty-schema.ts (optional career.dynasty); dynastyTurn runs once per turn (normal and prison) keyed by dynasty.lastPeriod, ageing dependents in the Q4 turn and billing the retainer; the manager multiplier is applied inside activityGains so previews match actual gains. attention-blocker.ts gates Next Turn on an unstrategized trial or respondable scandal.
- Legislators get a dedicated Government layout (status, approval, chamber, docket, then Past Roll Calls / Lawmaker Directory / Bill Archive drawers); the executive docket renders only while executiveOffice holds. Player bills need caucusSupport ≥ CAUCUS_MIN to be introduced; ensureLegislativeDocket files colleague bills alternating parties (cap 3 live) and alternates floor/committee priority by quarter. legislative-record.ts judges each legislative term once inside archiveTerm (zero passed bills → reputation penalty + career.legislativeRecord flag read by debates, town halls and rival attacks).
- Net worth (netWorthBreakdown in banking.ts) is the single formula for the dashboard and credit; loan caps derive from net worth + collateral with income only additive. bankingTurn tracks banking.missedStreak and forecloses (foreclose) at the threshold, stamping foreclosedOn so the credit penalty is time-bounded.
- Business growth (branches, annual marketing, GM automation, angel investors) lives in pure business-growth.ts; growthEffects feeds quarterlySimulation so forecasts match settlement, investor pitches roll deterministically per company/quarter/round, and owner stake scales dividends, liquidation proceeds and net-worth equity.
- Government finance (Phase 1) lives in government-finance.ts with cycle-free finance-schema.ts stored as optional treasury.finance, so it resets with each tenure: financeTurn runs once after treasuryTurn (lastEnd watermark) for economy-based revenue adjustment, keyed debt service, economy drift and the Q4 annual report; loans must mature within the term; tax changes reuse budget.taxes; annual project decisions delegate to approveProject and docket project choices sync via noteAgendaDecision.
- Citizen happiness (Phase 2) lives in happiness.ts and is stored as treasury.finance.mood; financeTurn moves it at most one step per quarter toward a factor target, applies approval momentum (zeroed by scandal/opposition when positive) and civic reactions; impeachmentRisk reads unrestQuarters.
- Quarterly events UI (Treasury → Events, turn-report highlights, history sheet) reads only quarterly-events.ts helpers (eventOutlook, responsePlan, freshEvents) so previews always match what respondToEvent/eventsTurn apply; tests that assert exact treasury settlement must exclude '|event|' transactions.
- Political Network uses network-schema.ts (zod-only, optional career.network) and pure network.ts: a static 30-figure catalog plus saved evolving state seeded lazily per character, a quarter watermark for networkTurn and per-figure turn markers/revision guards so actions and offers cannot replay; campaign endorsements from figures reuse campaign.endorsements with 'net:<id>' groups.
- Political appointments live in appointments.ts with cycle-free appointment-schema.ts (career.appointments, nullable for legacy saves). Superiors are network figures holding Mayor/County Executive/Governor or the sitting-President NPC; appointmentTurn (after networkTurn, lastEnd watermark) pays salary, penalizes unresolved dockets, resolves campaign pledges, and handles dismissal/term expiry. Agency spending uses the appointment's treasury allocation, never career.money; launchCampaign is blocked while a pledge or appointment is active.
- Household (Phase 6) lives in household.ts under life.social.household (normalized by householdOf so old saves default): harmony, child profiles keyed by dependent id, in-laws, per-turn family action marks, events and relative requests. householdTurn runs at the end of collectFamily with its own lastPeriod watermark; all spending goes through career.money + personal ledger, and passTorch applies heirBonus and carries businesses/owned housing.
- National legacy (Phase 7): legacy-schema.ts holds a permanent additive archive (evidence, executive records, life records, approval samples) keyed by a stable owner id; legacy.ts captureLegacy only records real outcomes and mergeArchives unions by id so imports, reloads and heirs never erase records; legacyMetrics derives scores/classifications from archive evidence alone. legacy-storage.ts mirrors the archive across careers on the device.
- Mortality (Phase 7): mortality.ts mortalityTurn makes one survival decision per quarter, seeded by owner+quarter hash and guarded by legacy.lastPeriod, so reloads cannot reroll; endResponsibilities archives terms before releasing office. Retirement/death lock political actions (campaign.ts checks dynasty.retired/legacy.death) while personal, family and business modules stay playable; passTorch/carryArchive carry the archive into the heir or a fresh career. Covered by src/test/legacy*.test.ts.
- Story Mode (Phase 1): story-mode.ts is a pure registry of scenarios with per-scenario feature matrices (available/locked/hidden; only available systems may run background ticks) and the persisted storyStateSchema (scenarioId, chapter, act, turn, decision flags). career.ts stamps mode ('sandbox' default, so legacy saves parse unchanged) and optional story state; story careers save under STORY_SAVE_KEY, never the sandbox SAVE_KEY, and the device-wide STORY_PROGRESS_KEY records completed scenarios additively. The Story Mode landing page reads the coin wallet read-only — no deductions, no secondary currency.
- Story engine (Phase 2): story-engine.ts is the pure, data-driven narrative layer — STORY_NARRATIVES defines acts, chapters (inclusive turn ranges + objectives), characters, starting variables/relationships and conditional events per scenario. advanceStory runs once per quarterly turn from Game's advance() (story careers only, after advanceTurn), resolving delayed trigger flags, deriving act/chapter and queuing at most one due event; completedEvents blocks one-time re-triggers and applyStoryChoice only resolves the currently queued event (replay guard), clamping variables 0–100 and relationships -100–100 and appending to the decisionLog. story-portraits.ts maps character ids to assets; story-card.tsx renders the animated post-turn modal and story-log.tsx is the read-only Story Decisions view under More. Sandbox careers never touch this module.
- Story content (Phase 3): "Enemy Across the Aisle" runs 4 acts / 8 chapters / 20 turns with Senator Vance (rival), Dana Cole (manager), Iris Malone (reporter) and Chairman Osborne (chair); event ids from Phase 2 are kept so mid-story saves never replay. Choices may declare career effects (approval/reputation/campaign momentum) as data; story-effects.ts applies them once per resolved choice through shiftApproval and the saved campaign, keeping story-engine.ts cycle-free (it never imports approval/campaign). Endings are data evaluated in order by evaluateEnding when the final chapter's last turn resolves (first match wins; the last ending is the unconditional fallback), stamped once onto story.ending and shown by story-ending.tsx; Game records scenario completion when an ending is stamped.
- Story marketplace: unlocks live in a device-wide key separate from saves and the wallet; purchaseScenario (story-mode.ts) saves the unlock before charging so a storage failure never takes coins, and free scenarios can never be charged.
- Story stage: story careers whose scenario has a SCENE_SCRIPTS entry (story-engine.ts) render StoryStage instead of CareerView and never run sandbox turns; scene progress lives in StoryState.session (nullable, so older story saves start a session on resume) and chooseScene rejects repeat choices so meter effects apply once.
- Scenario scene scripts (each with its own meters, cast and theme) live as pure data under src/lib/game/scenarios/*.ts (type-only imports from story-engine to avoid cycles) and register in SCENE_SCRIPTS; scenes link linearly via nextSceneId so a link-integrity test can catch breaks.
- Story stage endings (Phase 5): SceneScript.endings are pure data evaluated most-specific-first by evaluateStageEnding (last = fallback) and stamped once on story.ending when continueScene closes the final scene; discovered endings live additively in STORY_PROGRESS_KEY (endings per scenario, first date kept) and replayStory resets only the run, never device progress.
- Story screenplay layer: StoryScene setting/narration/castInRoom/stageDirection/beats are display-only; scenePrelude orders establishing + beats before main dialogue and the stage tracks the step locally, so saves and effects never change.
- Political network uses gradual discovery: figures carry an optional discovered flag backfilled by withDiscovery in network.ts; turn events, offers, notes and actions only involve discovered figures, and reputation reach (reachFor/reachLevel) gates introductions so the network grows one contact at a time.
- Party & Social Events live in social-events.ts (people pool, event catalog); attending only queues life.social.pendingSocialEvent, socialEventsTurn resolves it after the turn into life.social.introductions, and contacts join only when the player accepts — so no one is added instantly.
- Dispatch mail and Magazines kiosk live in dispatch.ts with zod-only dispatch-schema.ts (nullable socialMedia.dispatch/kiosk): mailTurn delivers contextual letters once per turn via lastTurn, replies apply effects once via replied, covers are awarded once by id and interviews once per year; springboard badges read unreadMail/unseenMagazines.
- HeartBeat lives in heartbeat.ts with zod-only heartbeat-schema.ts (nullable socialMedia.heartbeat): deterministic age/derived-trait/charm matching, persisted decisions/chats and revision-guarded Game requests; Ask on a Date introduces stable contact-p-* identities through relationships.ts then reuses Romantic Date accounting, with no bypass of dating/proposal/marriage/family progression.
- CrossiaPod podcasts use pure podcasts.ts/podcasts-schema.ts under nullable socialMedia.podcasts; podcastTurn runs in advanceTurn after tubeTurn with lastTurn and lastPaidQuarter markers so long-tail downloads and sponsor pay never replay.
- Audio: synthesized sound engine and settings live in audio.ts (Web Audio, no asset files); settings persist in device storage separately from career saves, and the engine unlocks on the first user gesture and suspends when the app is hidden.
- Audio cues: audio-cues.ts derives sounds by diffing careers inside one turn (Game.update) and never mutates state; turn advances emit no diff cues; dramaCues also spans turn advances but only for new/changed records, so reloads never replay stingers.
- Turn energy: career.actions stores energy percent. Costs, diet baselines, recharge tiers and thresholds are constants in cycle-free energy-costs.ts; advanceTurn refills to turnEnergyFor(diet). Coin recharges are pure in energy.ts (canBuyEnergy/rechargeEnergy), gated below RECHARGE_THRESHOLD, capped at MAX_ENERGY and never touch cash, bank or ledgers; the caller debits the device coin wallet only on success. parseCareer converts saves lacking energyModel (actions ×20) once. life.energy is a hidden vitality stat used only for burnout/stress drift; every energy display and action gate reads career.actions.
- Rewards live in pure rewards.ts with cycle-free rewards-schema.ts (optional career.rewards): achievement claims and mission completion are recorded on the career so they pay once; missions arrive at random per in-game year (seeded, no reroll), are filtered by the player situation (citizen/organizer/campaigning/official) and stay open two years; Results return coin deltas and only the UI touches the device wallet.
- Civil War campaign lives in src/lib/game/civil-war/ as scene-script data registered in SCENE_SCRIPTS; the first scene's choices set faction flags and branch, so it reuses the story stage without touching Sandbox.
